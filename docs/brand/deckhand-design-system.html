01

Essence

The look comes from the terminal: a character grid, flat ANSI colours on a mid-tone slate, monospaced type, box drawing and Deckhand's own glyphs. The product can be drawn in the same cells as the words around it, so it belongs to the frame.

The feeling is a quiet, competent tool: clear, plain, confident, a little playful for people who live in terminals, and understandable by someone who has never opened one.

Signature ingredients

  1. The character gridA 12 × 24 px cell, with faint dots on the ground.
  2. The dusk paletteMid-tone slate with flat ANSI accents: magenta brand, cyan selection, yellow attention.
  3. Monospaced typeMartian Mono headlines with a magenta block cursor; JetBrains Mono for the rest.
  4. Box drawing and blocksRound-cornered boxes, and half-block pixel art for the anchor and banner.
  5. Deckhand's glyphs⠋ ● ? ✓ ☐ ☑ ◆ ⎇ ✶ ◇ π ❯, with their product meanings.
  6. Yellow annotationPlain-language labels with leaders, and the real Deckhand UI.

Accents and states

Typical use

  • Magenta is the brand: the logo, the cursor and one or two accent words in a headline.
  • Yellow is attention and annotation: labels, leaders, rings, the "needs you" state.
  • Cyan is selection. Green, red and orange keep their product meanings.
  • The logo carries a one-cell drop shadow.
03

Grid

k1 · 12 × 24 k2 · 24 × 48
  • The cell is 12 × 24 px, the unit for sizing and placing type and UI.
  • Grid dots: a 1 px faint dot every 2 cells across the ground.
  • Scales are whole multiples of the cell: k1, k2, k3 …
  • Safe area: 4 cells (48 px) from every edge.
  • Gutters: 2 cells across, 1 row down, between blocks.

Notes

  • One glyph is exactly k cells wide: Martian Mono at k × 12 / 0.7 px, JetBrains Mono at k × 12 / 0.6 px.
  • Headlines are ink with magenta accent words, and end in a magenta block cursor while they're the active line.
  • Legibility reference (phone viewing, at the delivered size): headlines read well from ~72 px, other text from ~26 px; short lines, a solid panel behind text over busy imagery and about 2 s of stillness after text lands all help.
